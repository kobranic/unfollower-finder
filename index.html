Unfollower Finder

Hey girl! Welcome to the Unfollower Finder. This web tool will allow you to see who isn’t following you back because ofc we are not fans 😤 Check out the guide below to get started!

Quick Guide
  1. Open Instagram in a web browser (not the app) and go to your profile.
  2. Open your Followers or Following list.
  3. Click and drag to select the names — start your cursor at the very end of the last name in the list, so nothing gets cut off, then drag your selection up to the top.
  4. Copy and paste the whole block into the matching box below.
  5. Repeat for the other list, then hit compare.
